Sources: SEC EDGAR full-text search, the HHS OCR breach portal, Maine Attorney General breach notices, security news feeds, Mastodon #databreach, the VERIS Community Database (CC BY-SA 4.0), and leak-site claims aggregated by Ransomware.live. Claims are shown only as a tag on records that another source confirmed; a claim on its own is never published here.

Records marked unreviewed were produced by a fixed mapping or a language model and have not been checked by a person. Every stated mechanism carries a verbatim quote from its source; if the quote is not in the source, confidence is low. The data cannot tell a second breach at the same organization from late coverage of the first; two records with one name may be either.
