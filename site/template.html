Coverage and limitations

Records come from regulatory reports, organization disclosures, news coverage, and existing breach datasets.

Coverage is uneven. US healthcare and selected state reporting systems are overrepresented. Many sources omit the breach mechanism.

Multiple records for one organization may describe separate breaches or additional reporting on the same breach.

Glossary

Sources: SEC EDGAR full-text search, the HHS OCR breach portal, the California, Texas, Washington, Oregon, South Carolina and Maine state breach lists, the Have I Been Pwned breach list, security news feeds, Mastodon #databreach, the VERIS Community Database (CC BY-SA 4.0), and leak-site claims aggregated by Ransomware.live. Context joined at build time: CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog (via NVD) and daily closing prices from Nasdaq's data API, with Alpha Vantage for older filings. Leak-site claims appear only as a tag on records that another source reported; a claim on its own is never published here.

Records marked unreviewed were produced by a fixed mapping or a language model and have not been checked by a person.
